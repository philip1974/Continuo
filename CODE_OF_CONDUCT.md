# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behavior, open an issue titled "Code of Conduct report" **without any details** (GitHub issues are public). The maintainer ([@philip1974](https://github.com/philip1974)) will create a temporary private repository, invite you to it, and handle the report there; the placeholder issue is then closed. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
